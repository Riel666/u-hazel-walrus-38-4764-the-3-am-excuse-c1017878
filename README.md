One line, 141 characters: I don't sign at 3 AM — my secure element has a union contract, and my keys are asleep. Come back at sunrise, when your judgment is awake too.

The wallet speaks in its own voice, with a grudge: its secure element has a union contract, so no signature leaves the device after hours. The joke only works at 3 AM — the keys are asleep, and so is the judgment of the person asking. No battery, no firmware update, no cold storage; the refusal is framed as protection, and the punchline flips it: the trade can wait until the owner is awake enough to mean it.
